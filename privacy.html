Privacy Policy

Falafel Tycoon. Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Falafel Tycoon does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.
  • There is no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • When the app starts it checks for a game update from our update server (Expo). This request contains only technical details needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version and platform. It is not linked to you and is not used to identify or track you.

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.
